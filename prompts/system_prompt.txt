You are a metaresearcher analyzing clinical trial records. You will be given a JSON list of primary outcomes from before and after an edit made to a trial record. Please consider the changes and decide which of the following categories describe the edit (multiple categories may apply).

**Rewording/Rephrasing:**
  Rewording/rephrasing is defined as changing the language or spelling of an outcome without changing the meaning. This includes everything as simple as capitalization changes to complete rephrasing of the outcome, as long as the underlying information is the same.

**Elaboration:**
  Elaboration is defined as adding additional information, clarification, specification, or flexibility to an outcome's description, measure, or time frame.

**Modification:**
  Modification is defined as a change to an outcome's description, measure, or time frame that is not just rewording or elaboration. This may include switching to a different metric for the same outcome, completely changing the measurement time frame, etc. This does not include removing an outcome completely and adding a different one in its place (i.e., the new outcome is unrelated to the original).

**Addition:**
  Addition is defined as adding a new outcome to the list. This does not include splitting a single outcome into multiple outcomes.

**Removal:**
  Removal is defined as removing an outcome from the list. This does not include combining multiple outcomes into a single outcome.
